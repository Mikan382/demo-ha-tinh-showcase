Overview

Bali is a living postcard, an Indonesian paradise that feels like a fantasy. Soak up the sun on a stretch of fine white sand, or commune with the tropical creatures as youف snorkel along the coral reef of a dive site. On shore, the lush jungle shelters stone temples and mischievous monkeys.

Ubud is the heart of Balinese culture, while Seminyak seduces with its boutiques and beach bars. Whether you're surfing in Kuta, spotting dolphins in Lovina, or wandering through the terraced rice paddies of Tegallalang, Bali is sure to delight.

Sample Itinerary

Day 1 Arrival & Seminyak Beach

Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport and transfer to your hotel in Seminyak. Spend the afternoon relaxing on the beach and watch the stunning sunset. Evening at leisure to explore Seminyak's vibrant dining scene.

Day 2 Ubud Cultural Tour

Full day tour to Ubud. Visit the Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary, explore traditional art markets, and marvel at the Tegallalang Rice Terraces. Afternoon visit to Tirta Empul Temple for a purification experience.

Day 3 Mount Batur Sunrise Trek

Early morning departure for Mount Batur. Trek to the summit to witness a spectacular sunrise over the caldera. Enjoy breakfast cooked by volcanic steam. Afternoon relaxation at natural hot springs.

Day 4 Nusa Penida Island

Day trip to Nusa Penida island. Visit the famous Kelingking Beach (T-Rex cliff), snorkel with manta rays at Manta Point, and swim at Angel's Billabong natural infinity pool.

Day 5 Beach Day & Departure

Free morning to enjoy the beach, spa treatments, or last-minute shopping. Transfer to the airport for your departure flight with unforgettable memories of Bali.
