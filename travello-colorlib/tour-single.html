Tour Overview

Embark on the ultimate Bali adventure with this carefully crafted 7-day tour that combines thrilling activities with cultural immersion. From surfing the waves of Kuta to hiking the volcanic slopes of Mount Batur, this tour is designed for travelers who want to experience the best of what Bali has to offer.

Our expert local guides will take you beyond the tourist crowds to discover hidden waterfalls, traditional villages, and pristine beaches. Whether you're an adventure seeker or a culture enthusiast, this tour offers something for everyone.

Tour Highlights

  • Sunrise trek to Mount Batur
  • White water rafting on Ayung River
  • Snorkeling at Nusa Penida
  • Surf lessons in Kuta
  • Traditional cooking class
  • Temple visits with local guide
  • Waterfall swimming
  • Balinese dance performance

Day-by-Day Itinerary

1

Arrival & Welcome Dinner

Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport where our team will greet you and transfer you to your hotel in Seminyak. After settling in, enjoy a welcome dinner at a beachside restaurant with your fellow travelers and tour leader.

Dinner included Seminyak Beach Resort
2

Surf Day & Uluwatu Temple

Morning surf lessons on Kuta Beach with professional instructors. After lunch, visit the stunning clifftop Uluwatu Temple and stay for the mesmerizing Kecak fire dance performance at sunset.

Breakfast & Lunch Seminyak Beach Resort
3

Mount Batur Sunrise Trek

Early 2 AM departure for Mount Batur. Trek to the summit (1717m) with headlamps and reach the top for a breathtaking sunrise over Lake Batur. Breakfast cooked by volcanic steam! Afternoon relaxation at natural hot springs.

Breakfast & Lunch Ubud Art Hotel
4

Ubud Cultural Day

Explore Ubud's cultural heart. Visit Sacred Monkey Forest, browse the art market, and walk through Tegallalang Rice Terraces. Afternoon Balinese cooking class where you'll prepare traditional dishes.

Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Ubud Art Hotel
5

White Water Rafting & Waterfalls

Thrilling morning of white water rafting on the Ayung River through jungle gorges. Visit the stunning Tegenungan and Tibumana waterfalls for swimming and photography.

Breakfast & Lunch Ubud Art Hotel
6

Nusa Penida Island Adventure

Full day trip to Nusa Penida. Visit iconic Kelingking Beach, swim at Angel's Billabong, and snorkel with manta rays at Manta Point. Return to mainland for farewell dinner.

Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Seminyak Beach Resort
7

Departure Day

Free morning for last-minute shopping, spa treatment, or beach time. Transfer to airport for your departure flight. Selamat jalan (safe travels)!

Additional Information

Fitness Level

Moderate fitness required. Mount Batur trek involves 2 hours of uphill hiking in the dark. Rafting is suitable for beginners.

What to Bring

Comfortable hiking shoes, swimwear, sunscreen, insect repellent, light rain jacket, camera, and a sense of adventure!

Age Requirement

Minimum age 12 years. Participants under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

Group Size

Maximum 12 participants to ensure an intimate, quality experience with personalized attention.
