Help & FAQs

How can I book my stay at Ke Go Retreat?

You can book directly on our website using the Book Now button, or contact our reservations team via email or WhatsApp for personalized assistance.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major credit cards, international debit cards, and secure bank transfers. For on-site payments, we also accept digital wallets.

Can I modify my booking after confirmation?

Yes, bookings can be modified up to 7 days before your arrival date, subject to availability and any applicable rate changes.

What is the cancellation policy at Ke Go Retreat?

Cancellations made 14 days prior to arrival are free of charge. Cancellations within 14 days may incur one night’s stay as a fee. No-shows will be charged the full amount.

Do you offer seasonal promotions or packages?

Yes, we offer exclusive seasonal deals, wellness packages, and honeymoon specials throughout the year.
